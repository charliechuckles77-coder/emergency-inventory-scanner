How to use

Export the CSV and open it in Excel. Copy its data rows, without the header, into the next blank row of the Inventory sheet, starting in column A. The ten CSV columns match Excel columns A–J. Enter minimum quantities in column M. Do not overwrite calculated columns K, L or N.

Important: Barcodes do not encode expiry dates. Check the actual packet. Use-by dates are food safety deadlines. Best-before dates relate mainly to quality.

Open Food Facts coverage varies, especially for non-food and supermarket own-brand products. Correct any lookup results before adding. Your product corrections are remembered on this device.
